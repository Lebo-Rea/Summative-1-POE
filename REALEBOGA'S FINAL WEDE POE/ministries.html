Our Ministries

Kings Covenant Christian Centre has various ministries that allow members to serve God, grow spiritually and contribute to the church community. Each ministry plays an important role in supporting the church and helping individuals use their gifts and talents to serve others.

Worship Ministry

The Worship Ministry leads the congregation in praise and worship during church services and special gatherings, creating an atmosphere where people can worship and connect with God.

Intercession Ministry

The Intercession Ministry is dedicated to prayer and standing in faith for the church, its members, families, leaders and the wider community.

Children's Ministry

The Children's Ministry provides a safe and welcoming environment where children can learn about God and the Bible in ways that are appropriate for their age.

Youth Ministry

The Youth Ministry encourages young people to develop a strong relationship with God through worship, biblical teaching, fellowship, discussions and youth activities.

Women's Ministry

The Women's Ministry provides opportunities for women to grow spiritually, encourage one another and develop meaningful relationships through fellowship, prayer and other church activities.

Men's Ministry

The Men's Ministry encourages men to grow spiritually, develop godly leadership and support one another through prayer, fellowship and biblical teaching.

Hospitality Ministry

The Hospitality Ministry helps create a warm and welcoming environment for church members and visitors, particularly during services, events and special programmes.

Ushering Ministry

The Ushering Ministry assists with maintaining order during church services, welcoming people, helping visitors find seating and providing assistance where necessary.

Outreach Ministry

The Outreach Ministry focuses on serving and connecting with the wider community through evangelism, charitable initiatives and other programmes that demonstrate the love of Christ.

Media Ministry

The Media Ministry supports the church through photography, videography, posters, social media and other forms of digital communication. It helps share church services, events and important information with members and the wider community.

Get Involved

There is a place for everyone to serve at Kings Covenant Christian Centre. Church members are encouraged to discover their gifts and become involved in a ministry where they can serve God and others.
