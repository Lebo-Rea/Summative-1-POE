Sermons

Growing in Faith Through the Word of God

At Kings Covenant Christian Centre, we believe that the Word of God plays an important role in strengthening our faith, providing guidance and helping us grow in our relationship with God. Our sermons are based on biblical teachings and are shared to encourage, inspire and equip individuals in their daily Christian walk.

Messages from Our Leadership

Through the ministry of Apostle Amo Matlou and Prophetess Brenda Matlou, Kings Covenant Christian Centre shares messages of faith, hope, spiritual growth and encouragement. Our teachings aim to help believers understand the Word of God and grow in their Christian journey.
