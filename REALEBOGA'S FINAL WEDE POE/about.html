About Kings Covenant Christian Centre

Kings Covenant Christian Centre is a Christian church located in Naturena, Johannesburg. The church is committed to creating a welcoming environment where individuals and families can worship God, grow spiritually, study the Word of God and fellowship with one another.

Our church believes in the importance of building a strong relationship with God through prayer, worship, biblical teaching and fellowship. We welcome people from all walks of life to become part of our church family and grow together in faith. Our slogan is “The Place Where the Covenant Between God and Man Is Realised.”

Our Leadership

Kings Covenant Christian Centre is led by Apostle Amo Matlou, together with his wife, Prophetess Brenda Matlou. Through their leadership, the church seeks to encourage spiritual growth, strengthen families and positively impact the surrounding community.

Our Mission

Our mission is to lead people into a deeper relationship with God through worship, prayer, biblical teaching, fellowship and service to the community.

Our Vision

Our vision is to transform lives through the Gospel of Jesus Christ and to build a loving church community where people can grow spiritually, discover their purpose and positively impact others.
