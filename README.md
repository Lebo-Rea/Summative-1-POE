KCCC Website Proposal Summary (Proposal)
The proposal outlines the development of a professional website for Kings Covenant Christian Centre, a non-profit church based in Naturena, Johannesburg. The website will provide reliable information about the church’s leadership, service times, ministries, sermons, events, outreach programmes, location and contact details. It will include Home, About Us, Ministries, Sermons, Events and Contact pages, together with contact and prayer-request forms and social-media links. The design will use the church’s royal blue, white and gold colours, with clear navigation, readable text and accessible layouts. The website will be developed using HTML5, CSS3 and basic JavaScript and tested on desktop and mobile browsers. Development is planned over eight weeks, covering research, design, development, testing, deployment and evaluation. The estimated first-year budget is R13 793, including the domain, hosting, development, maintenance, backups and contingency costs.




Website Development and Design Improvements (CSS)
The Kings Covenant Christian Centre website has improved significantly from its original basic HTML structure. In the early stages of development, the webpages mainly contained headings, paragraphs, images, lists, links and navigation menus. Although the basic HTML made the information visible, the website did not yet have a consistent visual identity. The content appeared plain, some elements were not aligned correctly, and there was limited spacing between the different sections. The original code also contained a few incorrectly positioned or unclosed HTML tags, which affected the structure and appearance of some pages. These errors were corrected before styling was added so that each webpage could have a clear and valid structure.
The website was developed into a multi-page website consisting of the Home, About Us, Ministries, Sermons, Events and Contact Us pages. Each page was given a clear purpose. The Home page introduces the church, the About Us page presents its background, leadership, mission and vision, and the Ministries page explains the different areas in which members can serve. The Sermons page provides spiritual content and a link to the church’s YouTube channel, while the Events page contains weekly programmes, outreach information and an embedded Google Map. The Contact Us page provides the church’s address, contact details, social-media links, service times and a contact form. Organising the information across several pages made the website easier to understand and prevented too much content from appearing on one page.
A single external stylesheet called style.css was created and linked to every HTML page. This stylesheet controls the design of the elements that appear consistently throughout the website, particularly the header, church logo, navigation menu and footer. Using one general stylesheet ensures that these shared elements have the same appearance on every page. It also makes the website easier to maintain because changes to the header, navigation or footer can be made once in style.css and will automatically appear across all the connected webpages.

The footer uses the same royal-blue, white and gold colour scheme as the header. This creates balance because the top and bottom of every webpage have a similar visual appearance. The church name is displayed in gold, while the address, telephone number and copyright information are shown in white. A gold border above and below the footer gives the page a neat and complete finish.

The website’s colour scheme consists mainly of royal blue, white and gold. Royal blue was chosen because it communicates trust, stability, dignity and professionalism. It is also a strong colour for a church website because it creates a calm and respectful appearance. White was selected to represent cleanliness, peace and simplicity. It provides contrast against the blue and gold and makes longer paragraphs easier to read. Gold was used as an accent colour because it represents value, excellence, faith and royalty. It appears in borders, headings, the slogan and important design details. The colours work well together because blue provides a strong foundation, white creates openness and gold adds warmth and elegance.
The main royal-blue colour used in the CSS is #002f6c, while the main gold colour is #d4af37. A softer gold, #e2c66f, is used for the slogan so that it remains visible without appearing too bright. The main page background uses the light colour #f8f8f6, which is slightly softer than pure white. This helps the white content sections stand out while maintaining a clean appearance. Dark grey colours such as #333333 and #555555 are used for body text because they are easier on the eyes than completely black text.
Two main font families were used throughout the website. Arial is used for paragraphs, navigation links, lists and form elements because it is simple, familiar and easy to read on a screen. Georgia is used for headings, the church name and the slogan because it has a more formal and traditional appearance. Combining these fonts creates contrast between headings and normal content while keeping the overall design professional. The headings are larger and use royal blue or gold, whereas the paragraph text is smaller and dark grey. This makes the information hierarchy clear to visitors.
The Home page was improved by centring the worship image inside a white content area and adding a gold border around it. The About Us page was divided into separate sections for the church’s introduction, leadership, mission, vision and ministries. The leadership image was centred and framed with a gold border. The Ministries page was organised into individual sections, allowing each ministry to have its own heading and description. The Get Involved section uses a royal-blue background with gold and white text to make the invitation stand out.
On the Sermons page, the introductory information, leadership messages, sermon links, images and Scripture were separated into organised sections. The YouTube link was styled as a clear blue-and-gold button so visitors could easily identify it. The Scripture of Encouragement section uses a royal-blue background with gold and white text, giving the Bible verse special emphasis. The Events page was divided into Upcoming Events, Outreach Programmes, Weekly Church Programmes, Everyone Is Welcome and Visit Us sections. The service times were organised into a list, and the embedded map was centred and given a gold border.
The Contact Us page was improved by separating the address, contact details, service times, social-media links and contact form into different sections. The form fields were given sufficient width, padding and borders so that visitors could easily enter their details. The Facebook and YouTube links were presented as blue buttons with gold borders, keeping them consistent with the rest of the design.
Overall, the website has developed from a collection of basic HTML pages into a more organised, attractive and consistent church website. The improved structure, shared stylesheet, inline page styling, coordinated colour scheme, readable fonts and carefully spaced layout all contribute to a professional appearance. The royal blue, white and gold colours now work together throughout every page, creating a unified identity for Kings Covenant Christian Centre. The final website is easier to navigate, more visually appealing and better able to communicate important information to church members, visitors and the wider community.





Website Development: From CSS Styling to JavaScript Interactivity
The development of the Kings Covenant Christian Centre website has been an exciting and rewarding journey, as I transformed the website from a primarily static design into a more interactive, engaging, and user-friendly platform. Initially, I focused on using CSS to enhance the visual appearance of the website by improving its layout, colours, typography, spacing, and overall presentation. I wanted to create a website that reflected the identity of the church while maintaining a professional and welcoming atmosphere. The combination of elegant navy blue, white, and gold elements helped establish a sophisticated visual identity that remained consistent throughout the different pages of the website.
As I progressed with the development, I introduced JavaScript to bring the website to life and create a more meaningful interaction between the website and its users. JavaScript allowed me to move beyond simply displaying information and enabled me to develop features that respond directly to the actions of visitors. One of the features I implemented was an interactive Daily Bible Verse section, which allows users to click a button and display different Bible verses. I also developed a contact form validation feature that checks whether the required fields have been completed and verifies the validity of the email address and optional phone number. The form provides immediate feedback to users, making the process of submitting their information more convenient and user-friendly.
Furthermore, I implemented a Read More and Read Less feature on the About Us page, allowing visitors to expand or hide additional information without having to navigate away from the page. On the Ministries page, I developed expandable ministry descriptions using dynamically created buttons. These buttons allow users to explore information about the different ministries at their own pace. I also created an interactive Weekly Programme Finder on the Events page, where users can select a day of the week and instantly view the relevant church programme and service times. These features demonstrate how JavaScript has improved the functionality of the website by making it responsive to user actions and providing a more engaging browsing experience.
One of the most challenging aspects of the development process was creating the security-related and URL configuration files, particularly the robots.txt file and the sitemap.xml file. Understanding how these files work together and how they should be structured required careful attention to detail. The robots.txt file was created to communicate crawling instructions to search engines, while the XML sitemap was developed to provide a structured list of the website's pages and their URLs. Ensuring that the correct URLs were included, that the XML structure was valid, and that the sitemap reference was properly configured was a challenging learning experience. It required me to pay close attention to file formatting, website structure, and search engine accessibility. Although these files are not directly visible to users, they play an important role in improving the organisation and discoverability of the website.
Despite introducing several interactive features, I made a deliberate effort to preserve the elegant and sophisticated appearance of the website. I ensured that the JavaScript functionality complemented the existing CSS design rather than overwhelming it. The interactive buttons, expandable content, validation messages, and programme results were designed to remain consistent with the website's navy blue and gold colour scheme. This allowed the website to maintain its professional appearance while becoming more dynamic and engaging. My goal was to create a balance between functionality and aesthetics, ensuring that the website was not only visually appealing but also practical and easy to use.
Overall, the transition from CSS styling to JavaScript functionality has significantly enhanced the development of the Kings Covenant Christian Centre website. Through the implementation of interactive Bible verses, contact form validation, expandable content, dynamic ministry descriptions, and a weekly programme finder, I have gained valuable experience in creating responsive and user-focused web functionality. I have also developed a better understanding of website structure, search engine configuration, and the importance of maintaining consistency throughout a web development project. This experience has strengthened my technical skills, problem-solving abilities, and appreciation for the importance of combining elegant design with meaningful functionality. The final result is a website that remains sophisticated and visually appealing while providing visitors with a more interactive and enjoyable experience.
